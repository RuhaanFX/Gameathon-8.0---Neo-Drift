# Custom 3D Models Directory

Any 3D file dropped in this folder (`.glb` or `.gltf`) will be automatically detected, auto-scaled, centered, and loaded by the game engine without modifying any code!

### Supported Auto-Loaded Filenames:

#### 1. Rover Vehicle
- `colony_rover.glb` or `rover.glb` or `colony-rover.glb` or `custom_rover.glb`
- *Auto-scaled to ~4.8m length, wheels aligned to terrain, shadows enabled, procedural boxes hidden.*

#### 2. Nomad Player Character (On Foot)
- `player.glb` or `character.glb` or `nomad.glb` or `custom_player.glb`
- *Auto-scaled to ~1.85m human height, centered, procedural blocks hidden, plays embedded walk/idle animations if present.*

#### 3. Salvage & Resources (Scrap, Power Cells, Fuel)
- `salvage.glb` or `resource.glb` or `crate.glb` or `scrap.glb` or `custom_salvage.glb`
- *Auto-scaled to ~1.4m and replaces the glowing procedural supply boxes throughout the wasteland.*

#### 4. Raider Enemies & Drones
- `enemy.glb` or `drone.glb` or `raider.glb` or `custom_enemy.glb`
- *Auto-scaled to ~2.0m and replaces the procedural raider drones.*

#### 5. Buildings, Towers & Skyscrapers
- `building.glb` or `building_1.glb` or `tower.glb` or `skyscraper.glb` or `city.glb` or `custom_building.glb`
- *Auto-scaled to ~110m, centered with base grounded, and automatically cloned across the distant horizon megacity and landmarks.*
