# Custom Textures Directory

Any image dropped in this folder (`.png`, `.jpg`, or `.webp`) will be automatically detected and mapped onto the 3D world billboard towers!

### Supported Auto-Loaded Filenames:

#### Holographic Sky Billboards:
- **Billboard #1**: `billboard_1.png` (or `billboard1.png`, `billboard_1.jpg`, `ad_1.png`)
- **Billboard #2**: `billboard_2.png` (or `billboard2.png`, `billboard_2.jpg`, `ad_2.png`)
- **Billboard #3**: `billboard_3.png` (or `billboard3.png`, `billboard_3.jpg`, `ad_3.png`)
- **Fallback / All Billboards**: `billboard.png` or `billboard.jpg`

*Tip: 2:1 aspect ratio images (e.g. 1024x512 or 512x256) look best on the mega-billboard screens.*

#### 2. Custom Celestial Sky & Skybox:
- **Filenames**: `sky.png` or `sky.jpg` or `skybox.jpg` or `skydome.jpg` or `space.jpg` (or `.webp`)
- *Automatically mapped as an equirectangular 360° celestial panorama onto the infinite 3D skydome.*
- *Tip: 2:1 equirectangular panoramic images (e.g. 2048x1024 or 4096x2048) give an ultra-crisp horizon and night-sky appearance.*
